Cite Verify – Citation Verification & False-Fact Detection

About the Project

Cite Verify is a web-based application designed to analyze research papers and academic documents. It helps users identify claims, verify citations, locate original research sources, and highlight potentially false or unsupported facts.

Key Features

1. Upload – Upload research papers and academic documents.
2. Extract – Extract claims, citations, and references.
3. Retrieve – Locate original cited research papers.
4. Compare – Compare claims with their original sources.
5. Verify – Identify unsupported claims and potential false information.
6. Report – Generate citation verification reports.

How to Use

1. Open the Cite Verify website.
2. Upload a research paper or academic document.
3. Allow the application to extract claims and citations.
4. Review citation verification results.
5. Check the generated report.

Project Goal

To make academic research more reliable by helping users verify citations, identify unsupported claims, and detect potentially false information.
